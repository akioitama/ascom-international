# ASCOM International — company research

Research date: 9 November 2026.

Primary source: the public website at https://ascomintl.com/, including its sitemap, robots.txt, page HTML, and the WordPress REST API (`/wp-json/wp/v2/pages` and `/wp-json/wp/v2/media`).

Logos and project photographs were opened and read. Identities below come from visible wording on those images or from the page text, not from an assumption that a logo equals a contract.

Nothing in this file was taken from Valmont, Oshkosh, SWARCO, Rosenbauer, Hill & Smith, or Larsen & Toubro.

## How the current site is put together

The live site is WordPress with Elementor. The public homepage is the page slug `ascom-international` at `/`.

A separate page at `/home/` is an unused WooCommerce fashion template (dresses, watches, shoes, placeholder testimonials). `/shop/`, `/cart/`, `/checkout/`, `/my-account/`, `/collection/`, `/new-arrivals/`, and the product URLs under `/product/` are the same demo catalogue. They are not ASCOM products and are excluded from the new site.

The only blog post in the sitemap is the WordPress default `hello-world`.

## Sitemap and robots

`https://ascomintl.com/robots.txt` allows general crawling, blocks WooCommerce logs, cart query strings, and `/wp-admin/` except `admin-ajax.php`, and points to `https://ascomintl.com/wp-sitemap.xml`.

The sitemap index lists posts, pages, products, SureForms forms, categories, and product categories.

No PDF documents were found in the sitemap or on the pages that were fetched.

## Divisions and how the old labels fit

The current homepage “Markets We Serve” has four divisions:

| Division | Published summary |
| --- | --- |
| Creative Solutions | “Boost your brand with strategic advertising and data-driven marketing that reaches the right audience.” |
| Construction | “Building durable, safe and sustainable sites using modern techniques & materials.” |
| Manufacturing | “Precision-driven manufacturing services with a focus on quality, reliability, and scalability.” |
| Technology Solutions | “We deliver reliable IT services and advanced equipments to help your business stay connected, secure & ahead.” |

Other activities are real, but they are not separate homepage divisions:

- **Road safety and road signs** are catalogues inside Creative Solutions. There is no `/road-safety/` page.
- **Vehicle work** is a set of project pages (fire trucks, foam units, water trailers, vehicle bodies, painting). There is no vehicle-customisation page.
- **Engineering** is its own page, outside the main menu, and part of its copy is contaminated (see below).
- **Road beautification** was not found.
- **Waste management** was not found as a service. The manufacturing page mentions waste recycling as a production practice. RWMC appears as vehicle painting and branding, which is not the same as a waste-management contract.

The new site keeps Creative Solutions, Construction, Manufacturing, Technology Solutions, Engineering, Road Safety, and Vehicle Customisation as public sections. Road Beautification and Waste Management stay in the database as inactive categories so they can be switched on when the company supplies a description. They are not given invented service copy.

### Manufacturing

Published text, shortened only for this note:

ASCOM says it operates its own manufacturing facilities and produces products “tailored for global markets”, with in-house control from raw material sourcing to packaging.

- Total area: “70,000+ sq ft (customize based on actual size)”. The parenthetical note is on the live page. The figure is stored for confirmation and is not shown as a verified fact.
- Production units: specialised divisions for metal, plastic, and electrical goods.
- Workforce: “100+ skilled technicians and engineers.” Also held for confirmation, not displayed as a verified fact.
- Capabilities: CNC machining and metal fabrication; injection molding and plastic processing; surface treatment (powder coating, polishing, plating); product assembly and private labelling; OEM/ODM customization; in-house quality testing and packaging.
- Responsible manufacturing, as stated: energy-efficient processes, waste recycling systems, ethical labour standards, minimal packaging waste.

The manufacturing page itself has almost no photography. Factory context comes from the project galleries.

## Code of conduct

The page “Ethics, Morals, and Legal Excellence” covers:

- A whistleblowing invitation to vendors and associated people, with a statement that identity stays confidential and good-faith reports are protected from retaliation.
- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption, including a zero-tolerance line and a ban on facilitation payments.
- Expected behaviour: follow applicable law, record dealings with officials, do not let partners or agents make prohibited payments, get advice before gifts or hospitality, and report concerns.
- Definitions of bribery, facilitation payments, and conflict of interest.
- A complaint form asking for name, company or vendor, phone, email, concern, evidence, and a message.

There is no separate anonymous platform URL. The new site keeps the text and routes a “Code of conduct” inquiry, with an optional attachment, to the same database as other enquiries.
